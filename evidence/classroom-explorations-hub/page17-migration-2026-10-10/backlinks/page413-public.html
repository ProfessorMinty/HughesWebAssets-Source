What We’re Exploring

Big Question: How do mushrooms grow, and why are fungi important helpers in nature?

We’re observing real mushroom growth over time—identifying parts of a mushroom (cap, gills, stem, mycelium), tracking changes, and connecting what we see to the mushroom life cycle and ecosystem roles like decomposition and nutrient cycling.

  • Observation skills
  • Scientific vocabulary
  • Cause & effect
  • Life cycles

Vocabulary & Fast Facts

Parts of a Mushroom
  • Cap: The top of the mushroom.
  • Gills: Thin sheets under the cap that release spores.
  • Stem: Supports the cap.
  • Mycelium: Thread-like network under the surface (the main body!).
Life Cycle
  1. Spore →
  2. Germination →
  3. Mycelium →
  4. Pin →
  5. Mature mushroom (releases spores!)
Safety Notes

We only handle our class kit. We never taste, touch, or pick wild mushrooms without a trained expert.
