              touch the ground. Its thick fur keeps it warm in icy winds. Musk oxen stand close together
              in a circle to protect their babies and block the cold. It’s like a living, fuzzy shield!
            </p>

              Each animal has special body parts and habits that help it stay safe and warm.
            </p>
