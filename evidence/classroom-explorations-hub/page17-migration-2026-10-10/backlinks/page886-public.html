🕷️ Spider Safe Zone

Learn about amazing spiders from the safety of your screen. No touching—just looking and learning!

  • Spiders aren’t insects—they have eight legs and two main body parts.
  • Spiders make silk to build webs, homes, and safety lines.
  • Most spiders want to hide, not bother people.
Start Learning ↓

What is a Spider?

ELI5: A spider is a tiny animal with eight legs and two main body parts (a head-chest and a belly). Spiders are helpers because they eat bugs that might bother plants or people. They live almost everywhere on Earth!

  • 8 legs (not 6 like insects)
  • 2 main body parts (no neck)
  • Makes silk from small nozzles called spinnerets

How Do Spider Legs Work?

ELI5: Spiders use body fluid (like “bug juice”) to push their legs straight—like tiny water balloons filling up. Then little muscles pull the legs back in. Push out, pull in. That’s their walk!

push out
pull in

This “push with fluid” system helps them climb webs quietly and quickly.

Web Wonders

ELI5: Spiders make silk threads with their spinnerets. Some spiders are like artists and build circle webs (called orb webs). Others make sheet webs or funnel webs. Silk can be stretchy and strong—great for bridges, blankets, and safety lines.

  • 🎨 Orb artists — neat circle webs
  • 🏠 Sheet makers — flat blanket webs
  • ⛺ Funnel builders — silk tunnels
  • 🧵 Safety lines — tiny seatbelts

Safety First

  • Look, don’t touch. Spiders are helpers—give them space.
  • Tell a grown-up if you see a spider inside your home or school.
  • Australia note: Some spiders there (like redbacks and funnel-webs) can make people feel very unwell. Adults handle those. Kids stay back.
