<!-- ======================= WHAT IS A SPIDER? (ELI5) ======================= -->
<section id="learn" class="sp-section reveal" role="region" aria-label="What is a spider">
    <h2>What is a Spider?</h2>
    <p><strong>ELI5:</strong> A spider is a tiny animal with <strong>eight legs</strong> and <strong>two main body
            parts</strong> (a head-chest and a belly). Spiders are <em>helpers</em> because they eat bugs that might
        bother plants or people. They live almost everywhere on Earth!</p>
    <ul class="sp-list">
        <li>8 legs (not 6 like insects)</li>
        <li>2 main body parts (no neck)</li>
        <li>Makes silk from small nozzles called <em>spinnerets</em></li>
    </ul>
</section>

<!-- ======================= HOW DO SPIDER LEGS WORK? (ELI5) ======================= -->
<section class="sp-section reveal" role="region" aria-label="How spider legs work">
    <h2>How Do Spider Legs Work?</h2>
    <p><strong>ELI5:</strong> Spiders use body fluid (like “bug juice”) to <em>push</em> their legs straight—like tiny
        water balloons filling up. Then little muscles <em>pull</em> the legs back in. Push out, pull in. That’s their
        walk!</p>
    <div class="sp-demo">
        <div class="leg"><span class="label">push out</span></div>
        <div class="leg back"><span class="label">pull in</span></div>
    </div>
    <p class="sp-note">This “push with fluid” system helps them climb webs quietly and quickly.</p>
</section>

<!-- ======================= WEB WONDERS (ELI5) ======================= -->
<section class="sp-section reveal" role="region" aria-label="Spider webs">
    <h2>Web Wonders</h2>
    <p><strong>ELI5:</strong> Spiders make silk threads with their <em>spinnerets</em>. Some spiders are like
        <strong>artists</strong> and build circle webs (called <em>orb webs</em>). Others make <em>sheet webs</em> or
        <em>funnel webs</em>. Silk can be stretchy and strong—great for bridges, blankets, and safety lines.
    </p>
    <ul class="sp-chips">
        <li>🎨 <strong>Orb artists</strong> — neat circle webs</li>
        <li>🏠 <strong>Sheet makers</strong> — flat blanket webs</li>
        <li>⛺ <strong>Funnel builders</strong> — silk tunnels</li>
        <li>🧵 <strong>Safety lines</strong> — tiny seatbelts</li>
    </ul>
</section>

<section class="sp-section reveal sp-safe" role="region" aria-label="Safety">
    <h2>Safety First</h2>
    <ul class="sp-list">
        <li><strong>Look, don’t touch.</strong> Spiders are helpers—give them space.</li>
        <li><strong>Tell a grown-up</strong> if you see a spider inside your home or school.</li>
        <li><strong>Australia note:</strong> Some spiders there (like <em>redbacks</em> and <em>funnel-webs</em>) can
            make people feel very unwell. Adults handle those. Kids stay back.</li>
    </ul>
</section>
