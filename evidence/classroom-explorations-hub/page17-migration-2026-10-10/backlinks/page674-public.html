The Butterfly Life Cycle

1) Egg

Tiny eggs are laid on host plants (like cabbage!).

Did you know? Parents choose plants that will feed their babies.

2) Caterpillar (Larva)

Chomp, chomp! Caterpillars eat and grow, shedding their skin in stages called instars.

Did you know? Those little black dots in our habitat are frass (poop) — a sign of healthy eaters!

3) Chrysalis (Pupa)

Inside the chrysalis, the body is re-arranged into a butterfly. It’s quiet on the outside, busy on the inside.

Did you know? Phil and Green Bean were our pioneers into this stage!

4) Butterfly

Wings pump up and dry. Then—lift off! We’ll release them outdoors where they belong.

Observation Log

Week 4 of Journey — Emergence & Release
  • All caterpillars completed metamorphosis except Green Bean.
  • Leafie wiggled and struggled at first, then emerged successfully. 🦋
  • We released the butterflies outdoors where they belong—see videos and photos above!
Week 1 of Journey — Arrival & Big Appetites
  • Students discovered caterpillars on brassica leaves with purple veins.
  • Lots of leaf bites and frass pellets observed (healthy eaters!).
Week 2 of Journey — First Chrysalides!
  • Philosopher Phil began chrysalis formation.
  • Green Bean followed shortly after.
  • Others continued feeding and preparing.
Week 3 of Journey — All Cocooned
  • All six are now in the chrysalis stage. ✅
  • Color note: some appear green near leafy, dimmer spots; others appear yellow in the bright, white room.
  • We’re watching quietly—next up will be emergence!

Words We’re Using

  • Metamorphosis: changing shape from caterpillar to butterfly.
  • Chrysalis: the protective case where change happens.
  • Instar: a growth stage between molts.
  • Camouflage: blending in to stay safe.

Try it at Home

  2. Draw your dream butterfly and label the parts.
  3. Keep a mini journal: What changed today?
